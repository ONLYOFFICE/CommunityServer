# **Welcome to ONLYOFFICE Groups 👋**

**ONLYOFFICE Groups** (formerly Community Server) is a free, open-source collaborative platform that acts as the central hub for your team. Manage documents, projects, clients, and emails—all in one place\!

Starting from version 11.0, Community Server is distributed as ONLYOFFICE Groups under the Apache 2.0 license.

## **Features ✨**

### **🗂️ Document Management**

* Organize and share files with flexible access permissions.  
* Integrate with popular cloud storage services like Google Drive, Box, Dropbox, OneDrive, ownCloud.  
* Embed documents directly into your websites or applications.

### **📊 Project Management**

* Create projects with milestones, tasks, and subtasks.  
* Visualize workflows with Gantt charts.  
* Track time spent on tasks and generate automated reports.

### **👥 Customer Relationship Management (CRM)**

* Manage your customer database, track contacts, and log interactions.  
* Generate invoices and track payments.  
* Create web-to-lead forms to capture new opportunities.

### **🤝 Collaboration Tools**

* Share articles and news through blogs and forums.  
* Build a knowledge base with an internal wiki.  
* Schedule events and meetings with a shared Calendar.  
* Aggregate multiple email accounts with the Mail client.  
* Maintain a central employee directory in the People module.

## **Part of ONLYOFFICE Workspace 🏢**

ONLYOFFICE Groups is the foundation of the complete [ONLYOFFICE Workspace](https://www.onlyoffice.com/workspace?utm_source=github&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=GitHubCommunityServer) productivity suite. You can enhance its functionality by integrating other components:

* [ONLYOFFICE Docs](https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/DocumentServer): For powerful online document, spreadsheet, presentation, and PDF editing.  
* [Mail Server](https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/Docker-MailServer): To create and manage your own corporate mail domain.  
* [Talk](https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/XMPPServer): For secure real-time communication via the instant messaging app.  
* [Control Panel](https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/ControlPanel): A central administration tool for easy configuration of your ONLYOFFICE Workspace.
